THE CONTEXT

SSTC / the problem

At the R&D and Service Center of SSTC Technology JSC, independently developed the warranty operations source from customer chat intake through shipping, warehouse reconciliation, inspection and return workflows.

From customer intake to inspection and returns: a Spring Boot workflow where the backend owns every business decision.

THE WORKFLOW

Follow the
business rule.

A simplified path through the system. Each step has an explicit responsibility.

  1. 01

    Chat Intake

    The customer describes the warranty request naturally; the bot extracts intent and product clues without forcing a rigid form.

  2. 02

    Verify Serial

    The backend looks up the serial in PostgreSQL, reads the authoritative product identity and asks the customer to confirm it.

  3. 03

    Collect Proof

    The bot gathers the issue, 1–5 condition photos, contact details, Zalo phone and pickup address, skipping fields already known.

  4. 04

    Create Ticket

    A confirmed intake becomes a ticket with an atomic daily code such as NS-YYYYMMDD-000001.

  5. 05

    Ship & Receive

    A provider abstraction creates and tracks shipments; warehouse staff scan labels, reconcile received components and retain evidence.

  6. 06

    Inspect & Return

    Role-controlled inspection, manager decisions, customer consent and return fulfillment remain traceable through append-only audit and evidence records.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

The interesting
part of the work.

The constraints, the trade-offs,
and how I approached them.

01

An LLM is useful for understanding language, but it must never decide whether a product exists, is still covered, or deserves a ticket.

+
THE DECISION

Claude is isolated behind an AiOrchestrator interface and only returns intent, confirmation and extracted fields. PII-bearing steps stay on local rules, and any timeout or malformed AI response falls back through Resilience4j to the deterministic mock engine.

02

Catalog values can be corrected later, but an issued warranty ticket must preserve exactly what was accepted at intake.

+
THE DECISION

Serial-first verification reads warranty and product identity from relational data, then snapshots description, model, SKU, product number and serial onto the ticket while retaining the component foreign key.

03

Rapid retries and duplicate messages can otherwise create duplicate tickets, active sessions or ticket codes.

+
THE DECISION

Client-message idempotency, optimistic locking, database uniqueness constraints and an atomic ticket-code sequence keep concurrent turns consistent. External AI and shipping calls are kept outside long database transactions.

04

A shipping outage must not roll back or lose a warranty request that was already accepted.

+
THE DECISION

Ticket creation and shipping application use separate short transactions. Idempotency and a shipping-state ledger make retries and out-of-order provider events recoverable.
